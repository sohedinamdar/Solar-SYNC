01 · The challenge

Sunlight moves. Fixed panels stay still.

Every hour changes the angle, intensity, and direction of available light. Solar Sync is designed around that simple reality: your energy system should respond instead of waiting.

THE OLD WAY

One angle for the whole day.

Traditional fixed arrays are optimized for a single installation angle. As the sun travels, the panel gradually moves away from its most productive position.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Turn motion into more output.

A compact tracking mechanism can continuously seek stronger light, reduce manual intervention, and make system behavior visible through live monitoring.

02 · The Solar Sync loop

A calm, closed-loop system for a moving sky.

Solar Sync combines sensing, decision-making, precise motion, and feedback in one clear workflow.

01 · DETECT

Read the light

Directional sensors compare relative intensity across the panel’s surroundings.

02 · ANALYZE

Find the sweet spot

Control logic translates sensor differences into an ideal azimuth and elevation.

03 · MOVE

Adjust with intent

Motors make measured corrections instead of constant, noisy movement.

04 · RECHECK

Keep learning

New readings close the loop and keep the array aligned throughout the day.
